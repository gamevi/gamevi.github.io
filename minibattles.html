💡 Pro Couch-Party Tactics

Execute the 'Bait-and-Hold' strategy. In fast-paced shooting or dueling modes, do not fire your projectile the millisecond the round kicks off. Instead, stay completely still or hold your positioning vector. Watch your friend's finger; the moment they impatiently waste their single shot, capitalize on their long reload or cooldown window to step forward and line up an un-dodgeable point.

⛔ High-Risk Input Trap to Avoid

The single most catastrophic mistake is rapid, frantic button smashing. Because every single input is heavily weighted by the engine to lock your movement trajectory or unleash an unchangeable action, spamming the key completely destroys your calculation tracking and causes silly self-eliminations.
