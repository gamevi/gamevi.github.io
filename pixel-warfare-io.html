💡 Pro Vertical Flanking Tactics

Incorporate 'Terrace Holding'. In retro multi-tiered maps, do not spend your match running down the main streets where sniper lanes intersect. Instead, utilize blocky wall ladders and staircases to reach high balconies and roofs. This allows you to look downward onto exposed paths, tracking running opponents while keeping your body safely concealed behind structural ledge layouts.

⛔ High-Risk Tactical Errors to Avoid

The single most critical mistake is running through corridors with your sniper rifle equipped in close quarters. If an aggressive opponent jumps around the corner with a shotgun or assault rifle, the high zoom factor and slow firing rate leave you completely defenseless. Always switch to your pistol or shotgun before entering enclosed structures.
