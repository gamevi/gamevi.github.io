💡 Pro Flame Zoning Tactics

Execute the 'Phalanx Hook' maneuver. When an aggressive player tries to run parallel to you to cut you off, don't just turn away. Instead, fake a retreat toward the boundary, tap your speed boost for a split second to loop backward in a tight tight hook shape. This flips your movement trajectory entirely, turning their own forward momentum into an un-dodgeable trap.

⛔ Dangerous Arena Pitfalls to Avoid

The single most critical mistake is overusing the speed burst during basic resource harvesting. Since dashing continuously drains your hard-earned mass shards, spamming it across empty map lanes actually makes you smaller, slower, and an incredibly easy target for observant predators looking for a quick meal.
